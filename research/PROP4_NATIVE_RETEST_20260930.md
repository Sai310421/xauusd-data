- Equity stop trigger: 3.5%, checked on quotes, with permanent halt for the remaining run. All profiles hit the stop. Observed DD slightly exceeds the trigger due to quote gaps and closing fills. This is an observed result, not a guaranteed 4% live cap.
- Native margin leverage: 30; base lot minimum/step: 0.01; 100 XAU units per lot.
- Common commission overlay: USD6 per round-trip lot. This is a scenario assumption, not a verified prop fee schedule. Cashback excluded.
- Daily loss reset uses Europe/Prague and initial capital denominator. No specific prop firm is certified; other rule sets require their own validation.

- Signals: source-derived TickSmoother candidate, not original binary parity.
- Stop modes are low-dimensional drift/variance FP and CVaR heuristics. Neither is a solved HJB-QVI or full distributional MPC, and neither is certified optimal.
- No latency, slippage, or shock stress and no new OOS have passed.

## Accounting correction

The old shadow ledger used price difference times lots, while native orders used lots times100 units. The native CurrencyPair instrument confirms quantity in XAU units. The old ledger therefore cannot substantiate a USD1000-account return/DD claim. Prior +14.34% conversion is withdrawn as a validated estimate.

Emergency distance5.75 and basket offset0.8575 are **price distances**, not USD basket loss/profit amounts.

KPIs now use actual fill cash flows and account balance; gross basket P/L is reconstructed when native exposure returns to zero. Net return uses the native ending USD balance minus the explicit fee overlay. Dollar cent rounding is reconciled with bound0.005 times native fill count plus0.01; rejected orders, open exposure, inconsistent fill cash flow, or larger discrepancies invalidate the result. Shadow decision P/L is retained only for diagnostics.

A deterministic 300-quote smoke passed with net USD0.86 without fees and USD0.80 with fees. Native/raw reconciliation errors across seven runs were USD0.002–1.258, within native per-fill rounding bounds.

## Decision and next research

Do not promote the stopping heuristic. On USD1000, the budget controller reduced the loss to about USD7.51 but did not create positive expectancy. Layer9 stopping never fired on the small account, because the equity stop arrived at shallow layers. Candidate research should address early-layer exposure and the 0.01-lot minimum, then validate on non-overlapping OOS after selecting on development data. Larger capital alone does not establish the requested profitability.
